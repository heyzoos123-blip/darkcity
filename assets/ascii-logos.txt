===========================================
• Concept 1 (BRUTAL BLOCK) - Best for headers, banners, maximum impact

• Concept 6 (WEAPONS + ASCII) - Most thematic for bloodsport
• Concept 7 (ULTRA MINIMAL) - Signature/branding

RECOMMENDED: Use Concept 4 or 7 as primary logo (clean ASCII)
             Use Concept 6 for arena/combat themed content

Colors when rendered:
- Main text: #ff0000 (blood red) or #00ff00 (matrix green)
- Glow: matching neon shadow
- Background: #000000 or #0a0a0a
