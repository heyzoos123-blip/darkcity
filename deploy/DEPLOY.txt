DARKCITY DEPLOYMENT - SIMPLE INSTRUCTIONS

1. Go to: https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Drag the entire "deploy" folder onto the page
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. Copy the Netlify URL you get (something like https://randomname.netlify.app)
5. Go to Domain settings on that new site
6. Remove darkcity.wtf from darkflobi.com site
7. Add darkcity.wtf to the new DARKCITY site

OR EVEN SIMPLER:

1. In Netlify, click "Add new site" → "Deploy manually"
2. Drag the "deploy" folder
3. Once deployed, go to Domain management
4. Add darkcity.wtf as custom domain

That's it - DARKCITY goes live! 💀
