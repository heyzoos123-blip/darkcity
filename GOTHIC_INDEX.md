## 🎨 Gothic Design System - At a Glance

### Colors
- **Primary:** #8b0000 (Blood Red)
- **Secondary:** #d4af37 (Antique Gold)
- **Highlight:** #ffa500 (Torch Amber)
- **Background:** #0a0a14 (Purple-Black)
- **Text:** #e8dcc4 (Aged Parchment)

### Fonts
- **Headers:** Cinzel (gothic serif)
- **Body:** EB Garamond (elegant serif)
- **Mono:** Courier

### Key Elements
- Stone textures (not glass)
- Warm candlelight glow (not neon)
- Ornate filigree borders
- Gothic symbols (cross, raven, skull)
- Weathered parchment cards
- Carved stone buttons

## 🎨 Key Design Principles

1. **Texture Over Flat** - Everything has depth and weathering
2. **Warm Glow Over Neon** - Candlelight and torches, not electric lights
3. **Ornate Over Minimal** - Victorian excess, decorative elements
4. **Serif Over Sans-Serif** - Classical typography throughout
5. **Shadows Over Highlights** - Drama through darkness

---

## 🌟 Visual Transformation Summary

**From:**
- Neon green/pink (#00ff88, #ff00aa)
- Glass morphism
- Sans-serif fonts (Space Grotesk, Inter)
- Sharp geometric icons
- Bright glowing effects
- Pure black backgrounds
- Modern, high-tech aesthetic

**To:**
- Blood red/antique gold (#8b0000, #d4af37)
- Stone and parchment textures
- Gothic serif fonts (Cinzel, EB Garamond)
- Ornate gothic symbols
- Warm candlelight glows
- Purple-black textured backgrounds
- Victorian, supernatural aesthetic
