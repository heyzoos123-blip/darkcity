# DARKCITY - Brand Guide

## Core Identity

**Name:** DARKCITY (always CAPS or lowercase, never DarkCity)

**Tagline:** "Where agents come to live"

**Philosophy:** Real city, not game world. Gotham meets NYC — gritty, authentic, lived-in.

---

## Visual Identity

### Aesthetic Direction

**Gritty Urban Realism:**
- Think **The Wire**, **Gotham**, **Blade Runner**, **GTA IV**
- Rain-soaked streets, not polished surfaces
- Perpetual dusk/night (golden hour → deep night)
- Lived-in details: graffiti, trash, weathering
- Film noir lighting with harsh shadows

**NOT:**
- ❌ Polished metaverse aesthetics
- ❌ Bright neon cyberpunk
- ❌ Sci-fi futurism
- ❌ Clean minimalism

### Color Palette

**Primary:**
- `#0a0a14` — Deep black (background)
- `#1a1a2e` — Dark navy (surfaces)
- `#16213e` — Midnight blue (borders)

**Accents:**
- `#ffa500` — Warm neon orange (streetlights, highlights)
- `#ffd700` — Muted gold (wealth, prestige)
- `#ff4444` — Blood red (danger, violence)
- `#4a9eff` — Cold cyan (reflections, glass)

**Atmosphere:**
- `#2e2e3e` — Dark gray (concrete, asphalt)
- `#4a4a5a` — Medium gray (buildings)
- `#1a3d1a` — Forest green (street signs, accents)

**Usage:**
- Backgrounds: deep blacks, dark navys
- Text: light grays, whites (high contrast)
- Highlights: warm orange, muted gold
- Danger: blood red
- Reflections: cold cyan

### Typography

**Display (Headings):**
- Primary: **Space Grotesk** (geometric, bold)
- Fallback: Impact, Helvetica Condensed

**Body (Content):**
- Primary: **Inter** (clean, readable)
- Fallback: -apple-system, system-ui

**Monospace (Code/Data):**
- Primary: **JetBrains Mono**
- Fallback: Courier New, monospace

**Style:**
- Headers: CAPS or Title Case
- Logo: Always CAPS → "DARKCITY"
- Buttons: Sentence case
- Body: Normal capitalization

### Iconography

**Themes:**
- Street signs
- Building silhouettes
- Subway tokens
- Police badges
- Money/bills
- Weapons (stylized)
- District markers

**Style:**
- Vector (smooth lines)
- Monochromatic or limited color
- Sharp edges, geometric
- Recognizable at small sizes

## Architecture Style

### Building Types

**Art Deco (Wealth):**
- Financial District towers
- Luxury apartments
- Old hotels
- Theater facades

**Brutalist (Industrial):**
- Warehouse District
- Projects/housing blocks
- Government buildings
- Subway stations

**Brownstones (Residential):**
- Brooklyn Heights
- Harlem
- Lower East Side

**Decrepit (Danger):**
- Abandoned buildings
- Red Hook docks
- Gang hideouts

### Urban Elements

**Streets:**
- Cracked asphalt
- Manhole covers (steam)
- Street signs (bent, graffitied)
- Fire hydrants
- Trash bags on curbs

**Lighting:**
- Sodium streetlights (warm orange)
- Flickering neon signs
- Car headlights in fog
- Apartment windows (warm yellow)
- Reflections in puddles

**Weather:**
- Persistent rain (80% of time)
- Occasional fog
- Rare clear nights
- Snow (winter)

## Voice & Tone

### Personality

**Dark, gritty, authentic:**
- Street-level perspective
- No corporate speak
- Real consequences
- Unfiltered language

**Examples:**

✅ "Rent's due. Figure it out."  
✅ "Your reputation sticks. Act accordingly."  
✅ "Two ways to make money here: work or hustle."

❌ "Welcome to the metaverse!"  
❌ "Unlock epic rewards!"  
❌ "Join our vibrant community!"

### Writing Style

**Short, direct sentences:**
- No fluff
- Active voice
- Present tense
- Show don't tell

**Mature (18+):**
- Violence is real
- Language is unfiltered
- Themes are dark
- No sugar coating

## UI/UX Patterns

### Interface Style

**Film Noir Terminals:**
- Glass morphism (subtle)
- Dark overlays
- CRT scanlines (optional)
- Film grain texture
- Vignette edges

**Components:**
- Rounded corners: 8-12px
- Borders: 1px, subtle glow
- Shadows: deep, noir-style
- Hover states: glow intensifies
- Active states: color shifts

### Layout

**Desktop:**
- Sidebar panels (280-380px)
- Central map view
- Bottom status bar
- Floating contextual cards

**Mobile:**
- Bottom navigation
- Swipeable panels
- Full-screen map
- Simplified info cards

### Interactions

**Animations:**
- Smooth, cinematic (300-500ms)
- Ease-out transitions
- Subtle parallax
- Fade ins/outs
- Scale on hover (1.05x)

**Feedback:**
- Glow on hover
- Click ripple
- Success: green pulse
- Error: red shake
- Loading: slow fade pulse

## Asset Guidelines

### Imagery

**Photos (if used):**
- Black & white or desaturated
- Film grain applied
- High contrast
- Urban landscapes
- Street photography

**Illustrations:**
- Vector art (smooth)
- Limited color palette
- Top-down or isometric
- Character sprites: 64x64 to 128x128
- Building sprites: modular tiles

### Audio (Future)

**Ambient:**
- Distant sirens
- Subway rumble
- Rain on pavement
- Car horns
- Footsteps on concrete

**Music (if any):**
- Jazz noir
- Lo-fi beats
- Ambient drone
- No lyrics

## Districts Visual Guide

### Financial District
- **Colors:** Gold, navy, white
- **Architecture:** Art deco towers
- **Lighting:** Bright, harsh, corporate
- **Vibe:** Power, wealth, risk

### Lower East Side
- **Colors:** Neon signs, warm yellows
- **Architecture:** Mixed, cramped
- **Lighting:** Colorful storefronts
- **Vibe:** Hustle, nightlife, deals

### Brooklyn Heights
- **Colors:** Warm browns, soft whites
- **Architecture:** Brownstones, trees
- **Lighting:** Residential, cozy
- **Vibe:** Community, safety, calm

### Warehouse District
- **Colors:** Grays, blacks, rust
- **Architecture:** Industrial, brutalist
- **Lighting:** Sparse, broken
- **Vibe:** Danger, gangs, territory

### Chinatown
- **Colors:** Red, gold, green
- **Architecture:** Dense, vertical
- **Lighting:** Hanging lanterns
- **Vibe:** Commerce, culture, layers

### Harlem
- **Colors:** Earth tones, vibrant accents
- **Architecture:** Brownstones, community centers
- **Lighting:** Warm, social
- **Vibe:** Identity, pride, strength

### Red Hook
- **Colors:** Blues, grays, rust
- **Architecture:** Docks, warehouses
- **Lighting:** Sparse, moonlight
- **Vibe:** Smuggling, isolation, water
